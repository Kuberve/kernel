- Use the name "Kuberve" to refer to the project itself.
- Distribute **unmodified** copies of Kuberve under the name "Kuberve".
- Write articles, blog posts, or reviews mentioning Kuberve.
- State that your product is "built on Kuberve" or "compatible with Kuberve"

- Fork the code for personal use, internal use, or open-source projects.

## What requires written permission

- Distributing a **modified** version of Kuberve under the name "Kuberve"
  or a confusingly similar name.
- Using "Kuberve" or the logo in the name of your company, product, domain,
  or app store listing.
- Using the logo in a way that implies endorsement or official affiliation.

**If you fork and modify Kuberve, please rename it.** Examples of acceptable
new names: "FooOS (based on Kuberve)", "Foo-Kuberve". This is a requirement,
not a request.

## Why this policy exists

We want users to know that when they buy a device branded "Kuberve", it
follows the official hardware and software quality standards. Forks are
welcome, but they must be clearly distinguishable from the original.
